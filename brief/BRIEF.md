30-SECOND ANIME SEQUENCE — "PERSEVERANCE"

FORMAT
- One 30-second video. 16:9. 24 fps.
- Three parts: an unbroken river take (0:00–0:13), one flashback (0:13–0:18), and a second unbroken river take (0:18–0:30).
- The video contains exactly two transitions: a ripple dissolve into the flashback at 0:13 and a hard cut back to the river at 0:18. There are no other cuts, edits, jump cuts or scene changes anywhere.
- Inside each river take the camera moves continuously and fluidly, as one long take. The timed beats below describe where the moving camera is at each moment. They are not separate shots.
- Slow, heavy, deliberate pacing. A cinematic film scene, not a montage or music video.
- Fang Yuan is the only person on screen. No other people, no crowds, no duplicates of him.

REFERENCE IMAGE
- The attached image defines Fang Yuan's character design: face, hair, skin, and the cut, material and color of both robes.
- Use it for design only. Do not copy its watercolor art style, seated pose, closed eyes, chair, branch, background or warm cream palette.
- Where this text differs from the image, this text wins: his eyes are open, he is standing and walking, the white inner robe covers his torso and shoulders, and the charcoal outer robe has slipped down below both shoulders and hangs at his upper arms.

VISUAL STYLE (whole video)
- Premium modern Japanese anime at feature-film quality, in the vein of ufotable and MAPPA.
- Characters: hand-drawn 2D, clean tapered line art, cel shading with soft gradients on skin and fabric.
- Backgrounds: detailed hand-painted matte art with deep atmospheric perspective.
- Water, spray, mist and light: dynamic and richly simulated, but fully stylized anime in the final look: sculpted foam shapes, crisp droplets with painted highlights, layered mist planes, soft bloom.
- Camera: cinematic lens feel, smooth stabilized movement, shallow depth of field in close-ups, gentle focus pulls, light film grain.
- Mood: dark, heavy, oppressive, inevitable.
- Never photorealistic, never live-action, never 3D-CGI faces.

FANG YUAN — PRESENT-DAY DESIGN (every river frame)
- Tall, slender young man. Pale skin with a cool undertone.
- Face as in the reference: long narrow face, sharp jaw, straight nose, thin closed lips, narrow dark eyes under straight brows.
- Hair: very long, straight, jet black, center-parted, worn loose. Soaked and heavy, streaming behind him.
- Inner robe: loose, pure white, cross-collar, wide sleeves, loosely open at the collar. It covers his torso and shoulders.
- Outer robe: dark charcoal-black with the faint deep-teal tint and soft mottled texture of the reference. Worn open, slipped down below both shoulders, hanging loosely at the upper arms, its long fabric trailing behind him in the current.
- Dark loose trousers and dark cloth boots. The boots are always under the water and never visible.
- Wet fabric is darker and heavy at the hems.
- No jewelry, weapons, armor or headwear.

EXPRESSION AND MOVEMENT (river takes)
- Neutral, emotionless face. Gaze fixed forward, cold and unwavering. Mouth closed. No smile, frown, grimace or tears.
- He is alive, not frozen: subtle breathing, natural weight transfer, a slight forward lean into the current.
- A steady, heavy, unhurried walk of roughly one step per second, without pause, through both river takes.
- Each submerged foot plants firmly on the riverbed. The step reads through the motion of his legs, the shift of weight in his torso and the burst of water around his shins. No sliding, no floating.
- He never stumbles, stops or looks away.

REVERSE FLOW RIVER
- A vast mythic river. Upstream, ahead of him, it disappears into mist with no end in sight. Dark jagged cliffs rise at the far left and right edges of wide views, empty of people.
- Direction, fixed in world space: Fang Yuan walks upstream. The current strikes the front of his legs and flows past him toward his back. In frontal views the water moves from the foreground away into the background, opposite to his advance. Keep this direction consistent from every camera angle.
- Splash may kick toward the camera, but the main flow never reverses.
- Water: deep ink-blue to black-teal, opaque, white-crested rapids, standing waves, sheets of spray, low mist on the surface. Knee-deep on him at the same depth throughout, bursting around his legs with every step. The riverbed is never visible.
- Sky: heavy slate-grey storm overcast with cold, diffused silver light. No sun, no blue sky.
- Faint pale-gold motes drift in the spray and are carried downstream with the current. They never react to him.

LIGHT AND COLOR (river takes)
- Cold, desaturated palette: blue-black, slate grey, steel teal, bone white.
- Dark and high-contrast, with readable shadow detail.
- Cool-white rim light on his hair, face edge, shoulders and robe edges, separating him from the mist.
- The charcoal outer robe always reads clearly against the background. The folds of the white inner robe stay visible until the final flare.
- Mist adds depth but never covers his face.
- In the river takes, the only warm color is the faint gold of the motes.
- Lighting, water level, weather and his design stay consistent from the first frame of each take to the last.

TAKE 1 — 0:00–0:13 — ONE UNBROKEN CAMERA MOVE
- 0:00–0:04: Extreme wide shot, lens just above the water, slight low angle. He is a small lone figure at frame center, walking toward the camera, upstream, through the raging river. The current flows away from the camera and past him. Waves break against his legs, spray bursts upward, mist drifts across the foreground. The camera glides forward low over the water toward him.
- 0:04–0:09: Without cutting, the camera arrives in front of him and settles into a backward tracking move at his walking pace, slightly low, framing him from head to the waterline. Five heavy steps against the current, water erupting around his shins with each one. Soaked hems trail in the flow. Hair and outer robe stream behind him. Rim light on his hair, face edge and white-covered shoulders. Droplets glint on his skin.
- 0:09–0:12: Still without cutting, the camera keeps tracking backward while slowly pushing in, from medium shot to close-up to an extreme close-up of his left eye and cheekbone. He keeps walking the whole time. Water beads on his skin; a wet strand lies across his cheek. His gaze never moves.
- 0:12–0:13: The rushing river reflects in his dark iris. A ripple crosses the reflection and spreads outward until it fills the frame. The eye itself never glows.

FLASHBACK — 0:13–0:18 — THE GRAVE
- Transition in: as the frame-filling ripple settles, it dissolves into this scene and the color warms into the flashback palette.
- Look: faded amber-sepia dusk, soft bloom, heavier grain, gentle vignette, soft painted texture in the background. Same anime line art and face design as the rest of the video.
- Setting: mountain wilderness, wind through tall golden grass, a fresh mound of earth with a simple wooden grave marker.
- Character: Fang Yuan's younger self, with the same face as the reference, younger and softer. Long black hair tied back. Plain, worn grey-brown traveler's clothes.
- He stands before the grave with fists clenched at his sides, crying openly: tears on his cheeks, lips trembling, shoulders shaking.
- Camera: one slow arc from behind his shoulder to a three-quarter profile, catching the tears in the dusk light. No cuts inside the flashback.

TAKE 2 — 0:18–0:30 — ONE UNBROKEN CAMERA MOVE
- 0:18: Hard smash cut back to the river. The take opens on a waterline close-up of his leading lower leg driving against the current. His boot plants unseen beneath the surface; displaced water bursts around his shin toward the lens. Same knee-deep water, same downstream flow. The cold present-day palette returns instantly.
- 0:18–0:20: Without cutting, the camera tilts and rises up his body, past the soaked robes and white collar, to a frontal close-up of his face: neutral, eyes forward, mouth closed, wet hair, storm sky behind. Unchanged, as if the memory never happened. He has not stopped walking.
- 0:20–0:25: The camera eases back to a medium shot and drifts around to a frontal three-quarter view, tracking backward with him. A faint pure-white light kindles inside his chest, glowing through his skin and the white inner robe. With each step it grows brighter, spreading through his silhouette and lighting the edges of his robe folds. The river stays violent at full force; the light catches the moving spray around him. His face and his walk do not change.
- 0:25–0:29.5: One smooth rising pull-back into a wide shot. The white light surges into a blinding flare from inside his body, radiating out through his skin, collar, sleeves and hair edges. Rays of light cut through the mist and spray. He keeps walking upstream, eyes open, expression unchanged. The river keeps raging around him.
- 0:29.5–0:30: The flare blooms the frame to pure white and holds white to the end.
