# World and style bible

## Visual style (whole video)

- Premium modern Japanese anime at feature-film quality, in the vein of ufotable and MAPPA.
- **Characters:** hand-drawn 2D, clean tapered line art, cel shading with soft gradients on skin and fabric.
- **Backgrounds:** detailed hand-painted matte art with deep atmospheric perspective.
- **Water, spray, mist and light:** dynamic and richly simulated, but stylized anime in the final look. Sculpted foam shapes, crisp droplets with painted highlights, layered mist planes, soft bloom.
- **Camera:** cinematic lens feel, smooth stabilized movement, shallow depth of field in close-ups, gentle focus pulls, light film grain.
- **Mood:** dark, heavy, oppressive, inevitable.
- **Never** photorealistic, never live-action, never 3D-CGI faces.

## The reverse-flow river (A, B, C, E, F)

- A vast mythic river. Upstream, ahead of him, it disappears into mist with no end in sight. Dark jagged cliffs rise at the far left and right edges of wide views, empty of people.
- **Flow direction, fixed in world space.** He walks upstream. The current strikes the front of his legs and flows past him toward his back. In frontal views the water moves from the foreground away into the background, opposite to his advance. Splash may kick toward the camera, but the main flow never reverses.
- **Water:** deep ink-blue to black-teal and opaque. White-crested rapids, standing waves, sheets of spray, low mist on the surface.
- **Depth:** knee-deep on him, at the same depth throughout. Water bursts around his legs with every step.
- **Never visible:** the riverbed or his boots.
- **Never happens:** the river slows, parts, freezes or turns still.
- **Sky:** heavy slate-grey storm overcast, cold diffused silver light. No sun, no blue sky.
- **Motes:** faint pale-gold motes drift in the spray and are carried downstream with the current. They never react to him.

## Light and color (river)

- Cold, desaturated palette: blue-black, slate grey, steel teal, bone white.
- Dark and high-contrast, with readable shadow detail. No crushed blacks.
- Cool-white rim light on his hair, face edge, shoulders and robe edges, separating him from the mist.
- The charcoal outer robe always reads clearly against the background. The folds of the white inner robe stay visible until the final flare.
- Mist adds depth but never covers his face.
- The only warm color is the faint gold of the motes.
- Lighting, water level, weather and his design stay constant from the first frame of each take to the last.

## The inner light (E and F only)

- The light is pure white and originates **inside his chest**. It glows through his skin and the white inner robe.
- It grows with each step, spreads through his silhouette and lights the edges of his robe folds.
- In F it becomes a blinding flare that radiates through his skin, collar, sleeves and hair edges. Rays cut through the mist and spray.
- **Not allowed:** a Gu creature, a glowing artifact, an external beam, a completed transformation, or glowing eyes.

## Flashback look (D)

- Faded amber-sepia dusk, soft bloom, heavier grain, gentle vignette, soft painted background texture.
- The line art and face design are the same as in the rest of the video.
- **Setting:** mountain wilderness, wind through tall golden grass, a fresh mound of earth with a simple wooden grave marker.
